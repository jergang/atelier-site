Una tradizione fiorentina

Florence and leather

Florence has worked leather for more than seven centuries. The leatherworkers' guild, the Arte dei Cuoiai e Galigai, was founded here in the thirteenth century, and the streets around Santa Croce still carry the names of the trade: Via delle Conce, Via dei Conciatori.

Downriver, between Florence and Pisa, the tanneries of Santa Croce sull'Arno still prepare the hides. Our workshop belongs to that tradition.

    Domande frequenti

    Questions

    Can I order a single bespoke piece?

    Yes. We make one-off pieces to order, from a first sketch or a reference you bring us.

    Do you produce under my own label?

    Yes. We make private label pieces and collections with your name, your labels and your packaging.

    Do you work with new brands?

    Yes. We work with emerging designers as well as established houses, and we can guide you from the first idea to the finished run.

    My brand is based outside Italy. Can we still work together?

    Yes. Briefs, samples and approvals can all be handled at a distance. Tell us where you are and we will plan the rest with you.

    Can I order a sample only, without production?

    Yes. A prototype is the first step of every project, and you can stop there.

    Which leathers and hardware can I choose?

    Calf, grained calf, suede, crocodile, lizard and python, among others, with hardware from chains and turn-locks to pearls and jewelled clasps. We help you choose.

    How many pieces, and how long does it take?

    It depends on the piece and the quantity. Tell us about your project and we will give you a clear answer.

    How do I start?

    Write to us with the form below: what you would like to make, how many, and when you need it. We reply to every enquiry personally.
