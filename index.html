Su misura

From the first sketch
to the finished piece

We design and make leather goods to order. A single bespoke bag or a full collection under your own name: we draw it with you, choose the leather and the hardware, build the prototype and make every piece in our workshop in Florence.

01 · Il disegno

The design

It starts with your idea: a sketch, a reference or a feeling. We turn it into a pattern, piece by piece, on the bench.

02 · Il prototipo

The prototype

A first sample is cut and stitched by hand, so you can hold it, wear it and change it before anything goes into production.

03 · Materiali e colori

Leather and colour

Every shape can be made in any leather and any colour.

04 · La produzione

Production

Once the sample is right, the same hands make the run, from one piece to many, under your name and with your label.
