Atelier Milano

Leather goods made to order for brands, cut and stitched in Milan.

We are a workshop that makes leather goods for other people's names. Bags, wallets, belts and gifts leave here cut by hand, stitched on the bench and finished at the edge, from a single sample to a full run.

Most of what we make carries someone else's stamp. This site is the first time we have shown it under our own.

Vitello

Bordeaux

Full-grain calf with a natural pebble. It takes structure well, so it is what we reach for in bags and anything that has to stand up on its own.

Saffiano

Blu Savoia

Calf pressed with a fine cross-hatch and sealed. It shrugs off scratches and water, which is why card holders and travel pieces are so often made in it.

Vacchetta

Zafferano

Vegetable-tanned cowhide, smooth and firm. It darkens with handling and keeps the marks of whoever owns it, so it suits belts, straps and gifts meant to be kept.

Soft, fine-grained and close to weightless in the hand. We use it for linings, pouches and anything that should fold rather than stand.

How a piece is made

  1. 1

    Taglio

    Cutting. The hide is read for scars and stretch before a single piece is marked, then cut by knife or by die.

  2. 2

    Scarnitura

    Skiving. Edges and folds are thinned so that seams sit flat and corners turn cleanly.

  3. 3

    Cucitura

    Stitching. By machine where a line must be dead straight, by hand where the machine cannot reach.

  4. 4

    Coste

    Edges. Sanded, painted and sanded again, coat after coat, until the edge is smooth to the nail.

  5. 5

    Timbro

    Stamping. Your mark goes in last, with heat and pressure, blind or in foil.
