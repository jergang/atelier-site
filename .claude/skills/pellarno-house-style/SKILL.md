# Pellarno house style

Reference: chanel.com and givenchy.com. Photography carries the page; the type stays quiet; almost nothing moves.

## Colour
- Base: ivory `#f6f1e9`, ink `#141210`.
- Full-width colour bands taken from the bags: burgundy `#5b1a26`, emerald `#0f4a3c`, saffron `#d9a21b` (ink text), fuchsia `#a3245e`, Florentine blue `#3d5f9e`. Ivory text on all but saffron.
- Never two bands of the same colour next to each other.

## Type
- Cormorant Garamond 500/600, uppercase, letter-spacing .1em to .26em: the name and every heading.
- Hanken Grotesk 800, uppercase, letter-spacing about .2em, 14 to 16px: labels, nav, buttons, captions.
- Hanken Grotesk 500, 18 to 21px: body. Keep text large and easy to read.

## Images
- Keep a photo's own setting where it is beautiful (tufted chairs, fur throws, velvet, workbenches, cutting mats). Cut out onto the paper backdrop only where the background is cluttered.
- Every photo is colour-corrected, warm-graded and sharpened; source in `/home/claude/grade2.py` notes.
- Captions name the material only. Branded factory photos live only in the "past production" archive.

## Copy
- Su misura (custom design and making) is the centre of the site.
- The houses are always in the past tense: "Houses we have produced for".
- One Italian label, one English heading, one or two plain sentences.

## Motion
- Only: a slow settle on the hero photos, a single fade-in for blocks, a solid header after the hero. No scroll-jacking, 3D, parallax or hover effects beyond underlines.
